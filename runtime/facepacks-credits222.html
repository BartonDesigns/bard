Bard modifications

Selected pitch/velocity zones; conservative silence trimming; level adjustment shared between velocity layers; selected crossfaded sustain loops; 32 kHz conversion; 112 kbps MP3 encoding; alternate-hit and drum-token mappings. Original paths, source hashes, gain values and loop points are included in the accompanying research/source/build222/sample-provenance.json.

Recorded alternate hits are used where available. Single-layer sounds are identified in the source notes; they are not represented as multi-velocity recordings. Original BARD chiptune sounds remain available.
