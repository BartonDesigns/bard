# Earth from orbit: credits

- Land imagery: contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2020, from the Sentinel-2 L2A 120 m mosaic
  by Sinergise (CC BY 4.0), as published on the AWS Open Data registry (`sentinel-s2-l2a-mosaic-120`).
- Sea, poles and gaps: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation, NASA Earth Observatory (public domain).
- Coastlines: Natural Earth 10 m land and minor islands (public domain).
- Night lights (`night-2k.webp`): NASA Earth at Night, as packaged in three.js's examples
  (`examples/textures/planets/earth_lights_2048.png`, MIT; the imagery is NASA's, public domain).

How it is made: `island/tools/bake-earth-imagery.md`.
