# Skin, October 15, 2026

The owner's note: "Can we get some actual layer of skin textures and shading so the hands don't look so flat?" The first-person hands read as smooth, warm, evenly lit plastic, and the bare forearm as a smooth tube. The MakeHuman skin maps give a hand about 130 pixels square of the 1024 sheet (under a texel a millimetre), so the detail has to be drawn, not painted. It is now drawn in the skin shader (`island/src/people/skin.js`) from a map laid on the refined hand surface (`island/src/people/hand-surface.js`). No texture is fetched and no geometry is added. Poses, grips, `viewmodel.js`, `held-items.js` and the sleeves are unchanged.

## What you see

**The layers, everyone's skin.**
- Light scattered under the skin wraps past the terminator, red the furthest (a per-channel wrap). The red channel sees mostly the surface under the fine detail, so pores and creases soften to a red edge, as they do in living skin. The wrap widens where the flesh is thin.
- Lit from behind, the thin places glow red-orange: fingers and their webs, the nail's free edge, the ears and the nose's wings.
- Two layers of shine. The broad one is soft. The skin's oil is tighter and brighter: oiliest on the T-zone, the knuckles and the fingertips, matte on the backs of the hands. Nails get a hard glossy lobe of their own.
- The sky's sheen. The world has no reflection map, so the hemisphere's sky and ground are reflected through both lobes. This gives a soft gleam toward the edges, and it follows the day and indoor light.
- Colour is a little uneven (blotches a few centimetres across), as real skin is.
- Close up, pores (0.7 mm) and the skin's coarser grain (2.5 mm, which still shows at conversation distance) break the highlight.

**Your own hands and forearms (first person, the fists and every weapon).**
- **Finger joints.** Palm side: deep folds at the finger's base, two at the middle joint, one at the last, deepening as the finger closes. Back: loose wrinkles bowed round each joint, which stretch flatter as the finger closes. The knuckles stand as domes, plainer in a fist.
- **Palm lines.** The heart line, the head line, the life line round the thumb's mound, the bracelets at the wrist, and fine lines between them.
- **Backs of the hands and forearms.** Fine crosshatched skin lines.
- **Tendons.** One from the wrist to each knuckle, standing as the fingers open, and the thumb's long one. On the palm side of the wrist, the tendons stand as the hand grips.
- **Veins.** The arch across the back of the hand, the veins down to it from between the knuckles, the two that leave it for the wrist, and meandering ones up the forearm. Each is a soft rise and a blue cast on fair skin, or a little darker on dark skin. They are plainer with age and on lean or muscular arms (`handLook`).
- **The forearm.** It is no longer a tube: the head of the ulna stands on the back of the wrist (the little finger's side) and the end of the radius on the thumb's side. The ulna's edge runs down the back, the muscles swell on the thumb's side toward the elbow, and long soft swells run along it. Body hair shows as a follicle and a fine hair per 1.7 mm cell, lying toward the hand. Past a pixel, only its tint shows. Hair is heavier on men, its colour the body's own stubble colour.
- **Nails.** A plate square at the free edge and curved at the cuticle, raised a little, with faint ridges along it. Pink over the bed, the pale half moon at its root, a paler free edge that light shows through, and the fold of skin round it.
- **Colour.** Knuckles and fingertips are redder (darker on dark skin). Palms are paler, much paler on dark skin, and so are the inner forearms. The sun has touched the backs of the hands.
- **Shade.** The folds between the fingers are baked per vertex. The sides of the fingers that lie against their neighbours are shaded too. The creases are a cavity term. Where the closing fingers and the palm meet the grip or fold into a fist, the shade follows how far each finger is closed that frame.

**Everyone else (NPC faces and hands):** the wrap, the two shines, the sky's sheen, the blotches and the pores. Desktops draw both pore sizes; phones draw the coarser grain only.

**Every skin tone.** Each effect is a multiply or a lighten of the person's own painted colour, keyed to its luminance where tones differ (palms, knuckles, veins, nail beds), so it works for every DNA blend (`people/body.js personDNA`) and the far worlds' painted skins (their light-through stays off). Tattoos, vitiligo, stubble, the painted scalp and the flush are untouched.

## How it works

- `hand-surface.js handAttributes(P, g)` lays the map on the refined hand geometry: three attributes, 36 bytes a vertex.
  - `handA`: which way it faces, the baked shade, how thin the flesh is, which finger.
  - `handB`: across and along the hand in hand widths and lengths. The hand's and forearm's frames are blended smoothly across the wrist.
  - `handC`: signed distances to each finger's knuckle, middle-joint and last-joint planes, and the nail coordinate.
  - The frame comes from the body's rest bones (`handFrames`). The back of the hand faces away from the thumb.
- `refineHandSurface` makes the three attributes blank, so the renderer binds them from the first frame. The material fills them the first time the surface is drawn (`closeHandMaterial`'s `onBeforeRender`): by then the body is known and the sleeves have cut the surface. So `viewmodel.js` and `anatomical-hand.js` need no change.
- `bakeShade` is point-based occlusion over a sample of the surface (2.2 cm reach).
- `fingerCurls` reads how far each finger is closed from the bones each frame, with no allocation, into a 12-float uniform. The shader turns that into contact shade on the palm side, flatter back-of-finger wrinkles, deeper folds and standing tendons.
- `skin.js`:
  - `RE_Direct_Skin` replaces three's direct lighting for skin with the wrap, the back light and the two GGX lobes.
  - The sky's sheen is added after `lights_fragment_end` with an analytic environment BRDF.
  - `heroSkin()` (define `SKIN_HERO`) draws the hands' detail into the colour, the roughness, a height for the bump and the shade.
  - `SKIN_LITE` (phones) leaves out the crosshatch, the hair, the forearm's fine swells and veins, and the palm's fine lines.

| Skin programs, desktop (crowd in the world, fists in the world, weapon view) | 3 (`crysis-skin-6`) | 3 (`crysis-skin-7`, `-7-hero` twice) |
| Skin programs, phone | 3 | 3 (`-7-lo`, `-7-hero-lo` twice) |
| All programs in the review scene | 18 | 18 |
| Weapon arm layer, tee (triangles) | 29,280 | 29,280 |
| Fists, per hand (triangles) | 14,640 | 14,640 |
| Hand map memory | 0 | 563 KB for the weapon arms (15,650 vertices), 282 KB per fist |
| Laying the map, once per build | 0 | 120 ms for both weapon arms, 85 to 260 ms per fist hand, on this machine's loaded software GL (load average 15 on 4 cores) |
| Value-noise lookups per pixel, crowd skin | 4 desktop, 1 phone | 7 desktop, 4 phone |
| Value-noise lookups per pixel, your hands | 4 | about 18 desktop, about 13 phone, plus 7 segment distances |

A tier change compiles nothing. The governor's tier (`qualityNow()`, `onQuality`) scales the fine detail through one uniform shared by every skin material: high 1, medium 0.85, low 0.6, minimal 0.35. Pores stop at 8 m on desktops and 4 m on phones. Draw calls are unchanged.

```sh
cd island
node_modules/.bin/esbuild tools/skin-preview.mjs --bundle --format=esm --outfile=dist/skin-preview.js
SKIN_OUT=/some/dir node tools/skin-browser.mjs
```

`SKIN_BUNDLE` loads another build of the preview (a BEFORE build). `SKIN_TONES`, `SKIN_LIGHTS`, `SKIN_VIEWS` and `SKIN_SHOTS` narrow what is captured. `skinReview.debug('vec3(skinCav)')` shows any of the shader's values as colour, and `skinReview.cost()` reports the programs and the map's size and time.
