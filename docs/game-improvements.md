# The Bard Chronicle

> The world does not stand still.

This is the plain-language record of improvements players can already meet in Bard. It feeds the **Chronicle** tab on the loading screen. Entries describe merged work, not plans, experiments or promises. Newest entries stay first.

Last woven: **October 22, 2026**
Source checkpoint: `d5307d6c5659a49df9f60115be5260d8dc5e6aae`

## October 22, 2026

<!-- 2026-10-22-world-finds-the-beat -->
### The world finds the beat

Play through the faceplate and the people around you answer with their own movement. Crowds, companions and your hero now dance with the pulse instead of beside it.

- Movement follows the heard beat.
- Different people keep different grooves.

<!-- 2026-10-22-cars-wear-their-years -->
### Every car wears its years

The roads no longer share one perfect showroom shine. Daily drivers, sun-faded paint, dust, deep gloss and matte wraps give each passing car a life of its own.

- A finish is chosen for each car.
- Reflections are softer and more believable.

<!-- 2026-10-22-law-reads-the-danger -->
### The law reads the danger

When a visible monster threatens a street, officers recognize the greater danger before turning on nearby citizens. Trouble now draws a more believable response.

- Visible monsters take priority.
- Local encounters remain free to unfold.

<!-- 2026-10-22-phones-breathe-easier -->
### Phones breathe easier

Large menus and idle layers now give the world more room while they are open. Returning from the spell book or music page is lighter and less likely to lose the scene.

- Less full-screen memory pressure.
- World buffers return when panels open.

<!-- 2026-10-22-road-between-moon-and-earth -->
### A truer road from Moon to Earth

The journey home now begins above the planet and lets the ground gather beneath you during descent. It is another step toward one unbroken universe.

- Earth builds during the approach.
- The surface streams in beneath descent.

<!-- 2026-10-22-gargantua-over-the-horizon -->
### Gargantua rises over the horizon

The black hole now carries the scale of a world: a vast dark body and a turning disc sweeping out across the distance instead of a small ornament in the sky.

- A planet-scale silhouette.
- A broader, spinning accretion disc.

<!-- 2026-10-22-world-returns-after-a-fall -->
### The world remembers how to return

If the browser sheds its graphics or a room reloads, Bard now has more ways to redraw the land, rejoin the last room and try again at a lighter setting.

- World redraw after a graphics reset.
- Last-room return and clearer recovery.
