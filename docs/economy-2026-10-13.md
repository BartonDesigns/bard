# The economy: named businesses, their signs, requests, payroll and the bank (13 October 2026)

Every town, gridded, mapped or grown anywhere on Earth, now has a working life. At least a
third of its buildings are businesses, each with an original name, a board on its front, its
awning colours and the things it puts out front, placed where they make sense. The businesses
ask for help (requests) and pay the people who work in them (payroll).

## What is where

- `island/src/economy/establishments.js` (pure, node-safe): about 80 kinds of business (cafés,
  bakeries, waffle shops, pasta places, pizzerias, diners, fast food with a drive-through, taco
  shops, sushi and ramen, pubs, barbecue, seafood, curry, noodles, tapas, izakaya, kebab; shops
  from bookshops to fishing outfitters, bait and tackle, surf shops, hunting posts, general and
  feed stores, farm stands; services; B&Bs, motels, cabins, hotels; tech companies, law firms,
  studios, clinics; breweries, garages, boatyards; and hospital, police and fire station). Each
  has weights by setting, a sign style (shape, lettering, colours), awning colours, props and
  name patterns. `localeAt(lat, lon)` picks the place's words and food (Texas leans to
  barbecue, Japan to ramen and izakaya, Spain to tapas, France to bakeries and bistros, Italy to
  pasta and gelato, Mexico to tacos, and so on). `establish(b)` decides one building;
  `planTown(list)` a town, names unique within it. `DENY` keeps real brands out.
- `island/src/economy/venue.js`: what bay/commercial.js builds inside a walk-in block, shared so
  the sign out front agrees with the fit-out (a café sign on a café).
- `island/src/economy/layer.js` (browser): reads the city's list each rebuild, plans the town,
  converts row houses on busy streets to shopfronts, recolours or adds awnings, places the
  boards (one canvas atlas, one instanced quad mesh) and the props (bay/lotkit.js), and keeps
  the registry: `near(x, z, r)`, `byId(id)`, `info()`.
- `island/src/economy/requests.js`: the request catalogue and board (shape below).
- `island/src/economy/payroll.js`: wages per shift and bonuses per task through `onPay(fn)`;
  businesses keep balances. People's money is the bank's (below).
- `island/tools/economy.test.mjs`: the share per town type, determinism, unique names, no
  brands, place-appropriate food, services siting, requests and payroll.

## How the share is held

A building is a business by a seeded roll on its position (`hashAt`), with a chance set by what
it is and where it stands (`chanceOf`): shopfronts, strip retail and offices nearly always; a
tower 92%; a warehouse 72%; a row house 70% on a main street, 35% on a corner, 12% elsewhere;
a house 80% on an arterial or in a grown town's old centre, 50% on a corner, a collector or
backing an arterial, 42 to 75% by the harbour, in open country or at the woods' edge, and 10%
on a quiet street (home businesses). The street is known from the grid's tags (bay/city.js:
collectors every fourth street, corners, corners on the arterial blocks), the mapped roads
(realcity near('roads'): primary/secondary/tertiary, corners where two streets meet, land use
7), or civgen's own tags (`b.street`, `b.core`, `b.artery`). The node test holds every kind of
town at 30% or more and quiet streets under 15%.

## Engine hooks

- `Crysis.economy()` counts, share near you, kinds, a sample of names, requests open, payroll
  totals, top earners and balances; `.near(x, z, r)`, `.find('pasta')`, `.requests()`,
  `.take(id)`, `.complete(id, outcome)`, `.payroll()`.
- Fares (`vehicles/fares.js`): two thirds of fares now go to a named business ("take me to
  Rosa's Pasta"), dropped at its door.
- Services: `registerSiting('hospital' | 'police' | 'fire' | 'armory', (b, setting) => bool | name)`
  claims a building before anything else (main.js registers the services runtime's chosen
  buildings, so their boards carry the service's own name); establishments carry `service`.
- The people's quests and money (main.js): `HOOKS.npcMoney.use(bank)`, the requests as a quest
  source (`fromRequests`), and the registry as their places (`HOOKS.npcQuests.establishments`:
  walk-in doors and tables from commercial.js named from the registry, the rest at their doors).
- `city.procHomes` skips houses inside a base clearing (`inClearing`).
- Payroll is wired into `people/workplace-life.js` (wage on turning up, a bonus per stretch of
  station work, a sale's revenue to the shop) and `people/workplace-routine.js` (a bonus for
  each meeting reached or stock check).
- Grown towns past the Bay: `city.update` used to stop outside the Bay frame, so civgen towns
  abroad were only built house by house within a few dozen metres. It now raises the grown
  towns anywhere (their buildings, kit and signs), leaving the Bay's grid, wild woods and
  skylines at home.

## The bank and the ledger

- `island/src/economy/ledger-core.js` (pure, shared with the Worker): `profileOf(household, member?)`
  turns a household (people/households.js) into its money plan (weekly pay and pension, help with
  children on low pay, rent capped to about two fifths of income, bills, daily food and living, the
  children's allowance, school lunches and clothes, a start balance and what it saves or loses a
  week, all from its seed and its work); `baseline(plan, day)` is its balance at the end of a day,
  closed form, the same on every device and on the server. `applyEvents` and `deltaAt` keep what
  actually happened on top. Kind codes: `KIND` (1 wage, 2 bonus, 3 sale, 4 request, 5 allowance,
  6 lunch, 7 clothes, 8 food, 9 rent, 10 bills, 11 gift, 12 theft, 13 robbed, 14 returned,
  15 hospital, 16 purchase, 17 tip, 18 transfer, 19 fine, 20 pension, 21 betrayed, 22 found, 23 other).
- `island/src/economy/bank.js`: `link(npcId, household, member)`, `household(npcId | person)`,
  `account(id)`, `balance(id, day?)`, `earn(id, credits, kind, counterparty)`, `spend(...)`,
  `note(...)` (a regular wage seen being paid: listed, already in the baseline), `transfer(from, to,
  credits, kind)`, `history(id)`, `daily(id, days)`, `standing(id)` (balance over a week's costs),
  `moneyTalk(npcId)` (mood, rent due and on what weekday, payday, what happened lately, how tempted,
  how they tip), `sync()`, `pull(id)`, `info()`, `flush()`. Account ids: `hh:<household>` (a family or
  a married couple of either sex: one account, both partners' pay in, the children's costs out),
  `hh:<household>.<n>` (a roommate), `npc:<seed>` (someone living alone, derived from their seed),
  `player`, `biz:<x>:<z>`. Stored per touched account: the delta, the delta at the end of each day
  it changed (90 days) and the last 20 transactions `[day, amount, kind, counterparty, base?]`, in
  localStorage `crysis-bank-v1` with a queue of events for the Worker. Payroll pays into it
  (`wirePayroll`).
- `server/ledger/`: the Worker (one SQLite Durable Object, a daily cron at 00:05 UTC) that takes
  clients' events, settles them onto the baseline, keeps each account's balance and daily
  snapshot, serves `/account/:id` and an admin page at `/admin` behind the `ADMIN_KEY` secret.
  Deploy: `cd server/ledger && npm install && npx wrangler secret put ADMIN_KEY && npx wrangler deploy`,
  then `localStorage.setItem('crysis-ledger-url', '<worker url>')` in the game. The game works
  without it.
- `Crysis.bank(id)` (the nearest person with no id), `.talk(id)`, `.daily(id)`, `.history(id)`,
  `.sync()`, `.panel(id)` (a chart, with `?admin` in the address).

## The shared request descriptor

```
{ id, kind, title,
  giver: { type: 'business'|'person', id, name, x, z },
  steps: [{ do: 'go'|'pickup'|'deliver'|'talk'|'wait'|'fix'|'chase', at: { x, z, name, establishmentId? }, item?: { name, shape } }],
  reward: { credits, from },            // credits 10..250, from an establishment id
  deeds: { done: 'favour', betray?: 'steal'|'robbery'|'kept-wallet' },
  tempt: 0..1, region, expires }
```

## Where bay/city.js changed

- `createCity`: makes the economy layer next to the props kit; returns `economy` and `economyInfo`.
- `fillBlocks`: tags only (no layout change): `H.street` on the gridded houses (from a `busy`
  value in the lot context: collectors every fourth street, corners, arterial corners), the
  shopfront awnings linked to their shops (`so.awn`, `o.awn`), the strip and mall stores'
  fronts (`front = -1`).
- `realBuildings(cx, cz, R, list, wild = true)`: the wild woods and creek trees only when `wild`.
- `update`: past the Bay frame it raises only the grown towns (no grid, no Bay woods or
  skylines); rebuilds when a grown town comes or goes; calls `economy.apply(list, x, z)` after
  the tree filters and before the size cap; `economy.setNight(nightK)`.

Also touched: `crysis/civgen.js` (houses tagged `street`, every box `core`, houses near an
arterial `artery`; layout unchanged), `bay/commercial.js` (its venue typing moved to
`economy/venue.js`), `bay/lotkit.js` (eleven props, meshes hidden when empty),
`people/workplace-life.js` and `workplace-routine.js` (payroll calls), `vehicles/fares.js`.

## Cost

One extra draw for every board (instanced, no shadow), up to eleven small prop meshes only when
something of theirs is near (each hidden when empty), awnings and drive-through lanes in the
city's existing box mesh. The atlas is 2048 x 1024 (128 boards, about 11 MB with mips) on a
computer and 1024 x 1024 (64 boards, about 5 MB) on a phone; boards are drawn once and kept
while wanted. Planning a town is a few milliseconds a rebuild (every 300 m).
