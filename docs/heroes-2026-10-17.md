# The heroes: toward the concept sheets (October 17)

Your body in the game used to be a random townsperson from a stored seed. It is now one of two
heroes made by hand, a man or a woman, chosen in the settings (**Your hero: Male / Female**) and
matched in passes to the two concept sheets. This note says what the characters are made of,
what was wrong with them and why, what was changed, how it was checked, and what is still short.

## What the characters are made of

They are **connected skinned meshes**, not primitives: the CC0 MakeHuman base mesh (19k
vertices, 64 bones with fingers and twist bones in the arms), shaped per person by morph
targets, dressed in clothes cut from the body's own cloth cage, with real assets for hair.

## Why they looked wrong (the structure, not the screenshots)

- **Inflated bodies.** The base mesh's only build targets are "max muscle" and "max weight";
  muscle fills every part at once, which reads as puffed, not strong.
- **Inflated, tubular clothes.** Every garment stood off the skin by one distance along each
  vertex's normal: trousers traced the knee and the calf like paint, and an untucked top hung
  straight down from the chest to an ellipse as wide as the shoulders (a box).
- **Bloused balloon trousers** came from an earlier attempt to fix that by adding room.
- **Culottes, the second time.** The first tailored cut (pass A) measured each trouser leg from
  the mean of the cloth cage's points at each height; the cage is coarse, a few points to a
  height, so that middle wandered off the leg and the reach measured from it ballooned (the
  knee came out 23 cm across, a knee being 11). Its ease was also counted twice (the fit's and
  the cut's), and the cloth was hung from the seat all the way down.
- **Sock boots.** Shoes were the foot's own cloth painted, with nothing modelled. The first
  built boot (pass A) kept that sock and added soles, laces and a tongue placed from the body's
  points of the shin, which include the cloth cage's: the laces stood 9 cm off the shin.
- **Flat vest.** The carrier's plates were straight bands; the straps peaked 3.5 cm above the
  base of the neck, standing over the shoulders like posts.
- **Strips at the temples.** The painted crop under short hair follows the cut's hairline,
  which comes down in front of the ear to below eye level; a faded cut blurs its edge over
  nearly 3 cm, laying a band of hair colour over the temple. A fringe card of an earlier
  hairstyle also lay flat on the skin by the eye.
- **Dashed black brows.** Brow hairs were drawn at 60% of the hair's colour (near black for
  dark hair) and, sparse, read as separate strokes.

## The inspection setup

`people/hero-view.js` (open with `?heroview`, or `Crysis.heroView()`): the same renderer and
materials as the game. Its studio draws the body alone on mid grey, under a soft sky and ground,
a key light high from the front left, a low fill from the right and a rim from behind, at a
fixed exposure; perspective views at 30 degrees for looks, orthographic views for proportions,
a flat grey override, the hair off, head, hands and boots framings, poses and actions held and
scrubbed, and what the body costs. Before and after captures use the same views.

## What was changed

### Pass A: silhouette and construction

- **Proportions** (`people/build.js`, `people/hero.js`): shoulders broadened with the arms carried
  out whole by their skin weights, the chest and back widening up to them, the waist drawn in, a
  smaller head scaled about the top of the neck, longer legs; a light muscle target and a **lean**
  setting (the weight target taken away, below the average body). Her build is her own, not his
  scaled.
- **Tailored trousers** (`garment.js` `trouserHull`): each leg's cloth laid over the leg's own
  cross-sections, measured from the line through its joints (hip, knee, ankle), bridged where
  cloth bridges (the back of the knee, the fold under the seat: each section's convex support),
  in quickly off the seat and then hung at the cut's taper, with room round the knee and more at
  its front, never past the body's middle at the crotch: fabric over legs, not legs painted.
  Measured across (him, her): thigh 21.8 and 17.2 cm, knee 17.1 and 13.6, calf 16.4 and 14.5,
  above the boot 14.7 and 14.2; read off the sheets, about 21, 16.7, 16, 14 for him.
- **Their construction** (`garment.js`, pattern `tech`): waistband and stitching, the fly's J,
  slant pocket openings, side seams and inseams, knees shaped by two seams and a dart with a
  panel between, a yoke and back pockets; bump detail, a little shade, a twill's unevenness.
- **Boots built, not painted** (`garment-kit.js` `bootGeometry`): the upper lofted from the sole
  to the top of the shaft, each ring round the convex outline of the skin at its height (the
  toes bridged, the hollows under the ankle bones filled, as leather lies), the toe box raised
  over the toes to its own height and rounded at the front; under it an outsole whose heel
  stands taller than the forefoot with the heel's breast between, a midsole set in above it,
  toe spring; a toe cap and a heel counter stitched over the upper; a tongue up its front,
  crossing laces, eyelets and speed hooks; a padded collar. Every point is weighted as the skin
  nearest it, so the boot bends at the ankle as the foot does. No cloth sock under it, and the
  boots are kept however far away the hero is (they are the feet). Everything built over the
  legs and feet now measures the skin only, not the cloth cage's points.
- **Trousers into the boots**: they end inside the shaft, a little fuller just above a tall
  boot's collar (bloused), narrowing into a lower one's.
- **A belt** with its loops and a buckle; **bellows cargo pockets** with flaps on his outer
  thighs; on hers, **zipped pockets** slanting down the thighs.
- **The carrier** made compact: shorter (it ends above the belt, the waist showing below it),
  as wide as the sheet's (armpit to armpit), scooped round the neck and clipped at the corners,
  webbing rows front and back, a patch, a drag handle, flatter pouches, the straps lying along
  the trapezius instead of standing over it.
- **Fuller arms** (`build.js` `arms`): the upper arm and the forearm filled out about their own
  bones, fullest mid-segment (the forearm near the elbow) and nothing at the joints: more arm,
  not more of everything (the muscle target puffs the whole body).
- **Tops tucked** (no box hanging from the chest), an overshirt's hang reduced (`drape`), knit
  sleeves pushed up as a soft gather (`push`), and an **open overshirt's own collar**.
- **Plaid** cut as cloth is: round the body unbroken to a seam at the back, round each sleeve
  and along it; her check a little bigger.
- **Her overshirt's front**: a placket down each open edge with its stitching, buttons down the
  left and their holes down the right, and a chest pocket with a buttoned flap.

### Pass B: identity and surface

- **The temple strips** had two causes. A faded cut's painted crop blurred a band of hair colour
  over the temples: his cut now has a crisp painted hairline (`swept`, no fade). And some hair
  assets leave the temple bare above the ear or lay their cards flat by the eye (short01 and
  tousled both did, side by side in the hero view): his hair is now the sheet's cut, medium,
  tousled and swept, over the temples and the tops of the ears (`grump`), which shows neither.
- **Brows**: groomed rather than drawn: a fuller ribbon of the brow's own tone along each arch,
  shorter hairs swept the one way over it, his a little lighter than his hair's numbers (they
  read near black) (`face.js` brows `len`, `tidy`, `fill`; `body.js` `browTone`).
- **Faces**: his heavy brow over hooded eyes, a straight nose with a defined tip, high
  cheekbones with a hollow below, a square jaw and a firm chin, a defined lip, a strong neck;
  hers with high cheekbones, a slim lifted nose, full lips, almond eyes, a soft defined jaw.
- **Hair**: his dark chestnut (colours are used as they are), hers deep auburn; a `swept` cut
  (longer, lifted and swept back, full short sides) to compare with the asset.
- **Skin**: her freckles in a band across the nose's bridge and the upper cheeks, light and
  sparse; his stubble as heavy as the sheet's (a hero's own, `hairkit.js` `stubble`).
- **Hands**: the relaxed stance uses the fingers' own cascade with only a touch of curl; the
  thumb at rest lies a little in toward the palm, so open hands no longer stick it straight out.
- **His top** stands off the trapezius enough that the shoulders never show through it as they
  move (`shoulderEase`; a passer-by's shirt keeps its fit).

### Pass C: integration

- **The sheet's actions** (`people/actions.js`): a sprint off the back foot with the other knee
  high, a leap with the legs tucked, a scout's crouch on one knee with a hand down, an open-hand
  fighting stance, a climber's reach; shown and scrubbed in the hero view. In third person the
  hero tucks their legs when in the air a moment.

- Your body is built in idle time soon after you enter the world, not when first needed. Each
  hero's hair is a fetch of about 0.2 MB (his 220 KB, hers 196 KB), the base mesh (2.9 MB) the
  townspeople's own, already loaded.
- **The small things kept indexed** (`garment.js` `accessoryGeometry`): every accessory piece's
  shared corners skinned once rather than once a triangle. The hero's accessory mesh went from
  about 32,700 skinned vertices and 2.3 MB to 7,100 and 0.55 MB, the same triangles and
  normals; the hero's geometry in all from about 4.4 MB to 2.7 MB.
- The hero view's switching test: each switch's cost, and the renderer's geometries and
  textures before and after several switches (nothing left behind).

## Hair on the head (everyone)

All hair floated a centimetre or so above its head, on every body. The head was measured over
every vertex its bone carries, which include the base mesh's hair-fitting helper, a shell 0.5 to
1.4 cm outside the scalp; hair was kept out to that shell, and most styles (grump, tousled, the
bobs, the long ones, most of the ponytail: 17 of 28) are tied to the helper itself, so they were
built on it. Now the head is measured on its skin alone (`hair.js` `skullOf`, the old envelope kept
as `shell` for a cap or helmet worn over the hair), the narrow directions round the crown are
filled from their measured neighbours (a flat 8 cm guess there had hidden under the helper's
points), a style tied to the helper is brought down by how far each tie stands off the skin
(`hairkit.js` `styleChunk`), and a long groom's first layer starts 6 mm over the scalp, not 9.
The innermost hair over the crown now sits 0.2 to 0.6 cm off the skin under it on the heroes and
the townspeople measured (0.5 to 1.6 before); the procedural cuts (crop, locs, cornrows, braids,
bob, long, afro) and the grooms sit on the scalp too (`tools/hair-seat.test.mjs`).

## Fluffy hair and brows on the bone (October 18)

Main now grows everyone's hair as fluff (`people/hair-fluff.js`): tufts of two crossed ribbons
cut into fine blades, gathered into brushed locks that draw to a point, shaded as drawn hair
(two tones, a sheen band, a rim). The heroes wear it too, no longer the asset sheets:

- **Him:** `swept`, medium length, tousled and swept back, over the tops of the ears and down to
  the collar, with two loose forelocks falling on the parting's side (brush option `forelock`).
- **Her:** `pony` tied at the middle of the back of the head, a long wavy tail that leaves
  the tie and hangs (`tieY`, `tailLen`, `tailLift`, `tailDrop`). Her hair is swept back off
  the forehead with no fringe, and two long strands frame her face to the jaw on each side.
- **Their brows:** on main's new brows, which follow each face's own brow bone and lie on the
  skin. They have the fine hairs, with their own density, weight and tone kept.
- **The sheen band:** now the hair's own colour and softer. A pale band read as grey paint on
  dark hair.

Round the ears: the head the hair grows on is measured without its ears, and each ear is its own
ellipsoid the hair lies over. The ears come from the skin the face's `ear-out` target moves: with
them measured in, their bump spread to the directions round them and the hair fanned out there.
The rule that keeps long hair off the face holds only in front of the face now; at the temples it
had stood the sideburns off the head in a slab. Sideburns grow short and down, flat on the skin.
Long hair, ponytails and braids fall over the collar, the shoulders and the back as the head turns,
from the same capsules the asset hair used (`hair-collide.js`).

Hair costs more than the sheets did: him 17,192 triangles (4,170 before), her 12,636 (7,154).
Their accessories are as they were (his 8,816 triangles, 7,100 vertices). Merging main in
brought its armour and combat gear, whose detailed plate carrier would have replaced his. His
compact carrier made to the concept is kept for the heroes (`compact`), and the militia keep
main's. Main's kit models are also packed indexed, like everything else on the accessory mesh.

## Checks

Unit tests: 993 of 998 pass; the five that fail fail on main too (the RGB manifest, nearby
tiles, Svalbard's flora, the regional vegetation refresh), bar one this work had broken and has
mended (the skin audit's count of the crowd's noise lookups: her freckles' unevenness now comes
from the noise they already draw). MAGMA 4242, TERRAN 77 and Earth each load with no console
errors. The hero view's own captures report none.

## The three largest differences that remain

1. **Surface.** The sheets are painted to the thread: worn, creased twill, scuffed leather,
   skin with its grain, folds where the cloth gathers. Here the cloth is clean and smooth (the
   knees' wear and the seams are in the shading, no folds in the shape but at the knee and the
   ankle), the boots' leather is even, the skin smooth at a distance.
2. **Heads.** The sheets' faces are particular (his chiselled, hers strong-browed) and their hair
   is soft, strand by strand, with flyaways. Here the faces are the base mesh's, shaped by its
   targets. The hair is now fluff in brushed locks, closer to the sheets, but its outline is
   shaggier than their painted locks and its strands are drawn, not lit one by one.
3. **Build.** His sheet is broader through the chest and shoulders, his arms heavier; hers a
   little fuller in the shoulder. Here both are athletic but leaner (the muscle target puffs the
   whole body, so the build is shaped instead, and only so far).

## What is still short

- The actions are held poses in the hero view; in play only the leap is used (in the air). The
  game has no crouch or climb for them to follow.
- Hands: the base mesh's, posed by curling the fingers only (no spread); fine at play distance.
- The boots' toe is a little long in profile; the trousers' cargo pockets are flat slabs.
- Her ponytail is fluff now, with the sheet's loose strands round her face. Hair that hangs below
  the head falls over the neck, the shoulders, the back and the arms as the head turns, not
  through them; it is pushed out of capsules, not simulated, so it does not swing round a
  shoulder of its own weight.
