# Services: hospitals, police and fire stations, armories (13 October 2026)

Every town now has its services, built into the shared engine rather than placed by hand:
the Bay's mapped blocks (realcity.js), its gridded towns (city.js) and any town grown on the
globe (crysis/civgen.js) all go through the same siting, plans, fit-outs, staff rosters,
patient flow and units. The code is in `island/src/services/`.

## Siting (`services/siting.js`)

Each kind of service has a lattice of cells (hospital 3.2 km, police 1.9 km, fire 1.7 km,
armory 6.4 km) with a hashed anchor in each. The service stands in the building nearest the
anchor that can take it: an office, store or industrial block (mapped kinds 5, 6, 9; gridded
kinds industry and plain), never a home, a school or a tower, big enough for the plan, no
taller than the service can dress, on ground that falls less than 2.4 m across it. Each
building is dealt one kind by a hash of where it stands, so no building is wanted twice and
the choice never depends on the order buildings arrive in. `siteFor(kind, anchor, buildings)`
and `siteServices(buildings, x, z, r)` are pure: the economy's establishment registry can call
them with its own list and keep the answer. A chosen mapped block's `grp` is replaced (no
`biz`), so commercial.js and the generic interiors leave it to the service.

The runtime (`services/index.js`) decides one cell at a time (wall-clock paced) within 1.7 km;
a decision reads the mapped boxes near the anchor and, in a gridded town, `city.fill` round it.

## Buildings

`services/shell.js` builds the building the street sees: the plan's own outside walls,
windows and floors (interiors/build.js levels built bare), so nothing jumps when the rooms
take over; and, always, the plinth and steps, the roof, parapet and plant, the upper storeys
of a taller block, lit signs with original names (`services/names.js`), the hospital's
ambulance canopy and apron, the fire station's sectional bay doors and the police garage
door (they open for you and for the units). One builder merged by material per building;
shells exist within 900 m (500 m on a phone). The rooms are built by the interiors through
`interiors.addSite`, like the landmarks.

## Interiors (`services/interiors.js`, `fitout.js`, `kit.js`)

- Hospital: walk-in doors to the waiting room and triage desk, ambulance doors into triage
  with a resus bed and stretchers, emergency bays (beds, curtains, monitors, nurses'
  station), operating theatre (table, surgical lights, anaesthesia cart, scrub sink), the
  pharmacy (counter, shelving, medical stores), a stair to the wards and the nurses' hub.
- Police station: lobby and front desk behind glass, squad room, chief's office, holding
  cells (bars, bunks, steel fittings), evidence room (shelving, locked rack), locker room
  with showers, and the sally port with cruisers and their light bars.
- Fire station: apparatus bay with lanes and big doors, turnout gear racks, hose rack, tool
  wall, the pole through the bunk room floor; watch room with dispatch radio, kitchen and
  day room, bunk room, gym, showers and lockers upstairs.
- Armory: guard post, war room with the map table, wall screens, comms desk, briefing chairs
  and podium; comms room with radio racks; supply depot (racks, ammunition crates, supply
  crates, fuel drums, medical crates, weapon racks, forklift-ready aisles); barracks.
- For the military's own buildings: `buildWarRoom(frame)`, `buildDepot(frame)`,
  `buildBarracks(frame)` (frame `{ W, D, door? }`) return plans; `serviceSite(key, plan, o)`
  in `services/index.js` wraps one as an interiors site staffed by the armory roster.

Engine extension points used (small, general): `addKit(types, soft)` in interiors/kit.js,
`ROOM_FIT` in interiors/furnish.js, `rm.floor`, `rm.paint`, `rm.lit` and car `kind`/`paint`
in interiors/build.js, work seats, `P.roster` and per-level navigation in interiors/index.js
`venue()`, `WORK_ROLES` in people/workplace-life.js and `WORK_TRIPS` in
people/workplace-routine.js.

## Staff and routines (`services/roles.js`)

Posts carry roles and groups (a doctor and a patient in the same bay). The roster by the hour
(day 7 to 19, night after) chooses which posts are filled: doctors, nurses, a receptionist, a
pharmacist, a paramedic while an ambulance is in, a surgeon while a grave patient is treated;
a desk officer, officers, chief, detective, evidence clerk, detainees; a fire crew of five less
those out on a call, a dispatcher, the crew in their bunks at night; a duty officer, radio
operators, officers, guard, quartermaster, soldiers, a cook at mealtimes. Workers walk their
trips (ward rounds, patient checks, coffee, cell checks, gear checks, comms checks, stores)
on the floor's own route grid. Names on duty come from `staffName`.

## Patients (`services/patients.js`)

Arrive (ambulance or walk-in), triage priority by severity (a child ahead of an adult as hurt),
beds by priority then arrival, care over time at a rate set by the doctors and nurses on,
a spell recovering, then discharge and the bill (40 credits plus 0.6 a second of care, capped
at 900). A child is never billed and never turned away; a patient who cannot pay goes home
mended and owes it; a patient with no bed for ten minutes goes on to another hospital. The
hurt come from fights (`combat.woundedNear`), cars (`vehicles/impact.js` through main.js
`injury`) and the town's own falls, burns and illnesses (background arrivals and calls).
Treating patients fill the emergency bays, recovering ones the wards.

You: knocked out within 3.2 km of a hospital, you come round at its ambulance bay, treated
for 120 credits plus your missing health (owed if you are short); hurt, the triage desk mends
you for credits; the pharmacy sells field medkits over the counter (a real inventory item).

## Units (`services/responders.js`, `routes.js`, `sound.js`)

Two ambulances per hospital (under the canopy) and up to two engines per station (in the bay),
present within 1.3 km or while on a call. A route is found along the streets round both ends
(mapped roads, or the grid's streets laid out the way drive.js does) by A*. Units slow for
corners, flash their light bars and sound a two-oscillator wail through `soundBus()`, panned and
faded with distance; they stop for anyone ahead and stop dead for a child within 12 m.
Ambulances fetch the hurt and admit them on return. Fires: combat/combat.js now asks
`services.dispatchFire(fire, crew)`; the nearest station's engine drives out and puts its crew
down beside the fire (the existing fire crew in combat/ai-squads.js), returning once it is out.

## Caches (`services/depots.js`)

The armory depot (weapons), the police evidence room (police), the fire station's bay (fire)
and the hospital pharmacy (medical stores) are caches of real catalog items. Taking one is an
inventory `trade` transaction from `depot:<id>` (looting; anyone on duty nearby sees it and
your wanted level rises unless `services.hooks.onLoot` handles it); bringing one is a trade that
pays 60% of the item's price. Stock comes back one item each 8 minutes; stock persists per
viewer in localStorage. Police stations and hospital pharmacies also appear in the gear
screen's sources (`world.worldSites`).

## The room's live things (`services/screens.js`)

In a built war room the map table shows the streets and blocks within 360 m, turned the way
the building faces, with you, the services near, the units and their calls; wall screens show
the duty board. Rooms get hanging signs. Radios crackle in comms, war, watch and desk rooms;
monitors beep in the wards while there are patients.

## Hooks for the other modules

- `services.hooks.charge(who, credits, record)`: a patient's bill through the morality ledger
  (main's `HOOKS.npcMoney` at merge; until then most patients pay from their own purse).
- `services.injury(person, cause, severity)`: a hurt person to be fetched or walked in (main's
  `HOOKS.npcHurt(fn)` can feed it at merge, beside the existing car-impact call).
- `services.hooks.onLoot(depot, itemId, witnessed)`: the heat for looting a cache.
- `siteFor` / `siteServices` (siting.js) for the economy's establishment registry.
- `buildWarRoom`, `buildDepot`, `buildBarracks`, `serviceSite` for the military's bases.
- `Crysis.services()`, `Crysis.serviceGo(kind, room?)`, `Crysis.serviceCall(severity, minor)`.

## Verification

- `node --test island/tools/services.test.mjs`: siting (kinds, sizes, order independence,
  every kind in a town), patient flow (admitted, treated, discharged, billed; unpaid owed;
  transfer), minors (seen first, care only rises, never billed, urgent never refused), routes,
  depots through real inventory transactions, every plan furnished with roles that have
  activities. The full suite has only the four failures that were there before.
- `node island/tools/services-browser.cjs <outdir>` (Playwright, SwiftShader) enters the world,
  goes to a town, sites its services, walks into rooms and saves pictures drawn in the page.
  At San Ramon (37.7799, -121.978) it sited 11 services in mapped blocks (hospitals, police
  and fire stations; no armory cell falls there), built the hospital, police station and fire
  station inside, showed the emergency bays, the cells and the apparatus bay with its engine,
  sent an ambulance out to a call with its siren on, and logged no page or shader errors
  (only the sandbox's blocked network requests). The services' own frame cost was about
  0.2 ms; a cell decision peaked at 150 ms before the gridded fill was capped at 700 m.
  The software renderer runs the whole Bay at well under one frame a second,
  so the pictures are a check of placement, not of look.
